SAR guidance

Sentinel-1 VV/VH backscatter is unaffected by clouds and supplies the structure hidden under them.

Cloud-mask aware

A rule-based cloud detector produces the mask that steers fusion to cloudy pixels and suppresses SAR speckle elsewhere.

Measured quality

Every run reports cloud cover, confidence from test-time augmentation and, with a reference image, PSNR / SAM / MAE.
